# Triptastic Holidays: social media

Content for LinkedIn (personal profile) and Instagram (Triptastic account), prepared every week by Claude and **published only after you approve it**.

## Your part (about 2–3 hours a week)
1. **Monday:** review the week's drafts that Claude sends you. Reply for each post with *approve*, an edit, or *reject* (e.g. "LI-1 approve, IG-2 change the destination to Kerala").
2. **Video clips (optional, ~15 min):** paste the Google Flow prompts Claude gives you into Flow, download the clips and upload them in the chat. Claude edits them into a finished Reel. See `brand/flow-guide.md`.
3. **Anytime:** send trip photos and client stories (with the client's permission). The more real material, the better the posts.
4. **Optional, 10 min a day:** comment on other travel posts on LinkedIn. It's the fastest way to get noticed.

## Where things are
| Folder | What's in it |
|---|---|
| `content/<week>/posts.md` | That week's posts and their status (DRAFT / APPROVED / SCHEDULED / POSTED) |
| `content/<week>/*.mp4` | Ready-made Instagram Reels |
| `brand/` | Brand colours, name and the facts/stories posts are allowed to use |
| `log.md` | Everything posted so far and how it performed |

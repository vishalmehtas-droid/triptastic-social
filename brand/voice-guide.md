# Voiceovers in your own voice with VoiceStudio

[VoiceStudio](https://github.com/debpalash/VoiceStudio) is a free desktop app (Windows, Mac, Linux, plus Android/iOS) that clones a voice locally on your computer. No coding: download the installer from its releases page. It runs on a normal laptop with no GPU, just slower. The first time, it asks before downloading its voice model.

## One-time setup (about 20 minutes)
1. Install VoiceStudio from https://github.com/debpalash/VoiceStudio/releases/latest (Windows `.exe` or Mac `.dmg`).
2. Open **Voice cloning** and record 20–30 seconds of yourself reading anything, in a quiet room, phone mic close. Save it as your voice.
3. Do a test in English and a test in Hindi, and check it sounds like you.

## Each week (about 5 minutes)
1. Claude sends a voiceover script with each Reel (`content/<week>/vo-<topic>.txt`), timed to about 20–30 seconds.
2. Paste it into VoiceStudio with your voice selected, generate, export as MP3.
3. Upload the MP3 in the Claude chat and say which Reel it's for. Claude adds it to the Reel, lowering clip sound and music under your voice.

Even better, record the voiceover yourself on your phone (Voice Memos / Recorder) and upload that. A real voice builds the most trust. Use the clone on days you can't record.

## Also useful
- **Dubbing:** VoiceStudio can dub a finished Reel into Hindi, Gujarati, Marathi, Tamil and more, in your voice. That's one Reel reaching several language audiences.
- **Transcription:** it can turn a voice note into text. Send Claude a voice note about a client trip and it becomes a post.

## Rules
- Clone **only your own voice**, or someone who has clearly agreed. Never a client's or a celebrity's.
- Don't use the cloned voice to say anything you wouldn't say yourself: it's your name and reputation.

## LI-2 · LinkedIn · Wed 9:00 AM IST · Expertise · `DRAFT`

4 things I check before booking any honeymoon, after 17 years of planning them:

1. Visa rules and processing time, before the flights. Never after.
2. Passport validity. Both passports need 6+ months left at the time of travel, or many countries refuse entry.
3. Pace. 6 nights across 4 cities is a schedule, not a honeymoon. Two places, done well, beats four done in a rush.
4. Travel insurance. One missed connection costs more than the policy.

Simple stuff. It's also where most of the stress I've seen comes from.

Save this if a wedding is coming up in your circle.

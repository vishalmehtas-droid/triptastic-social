Every November, the same message starts arriving: "We're thinking of going somewhere over Christmas. Is it too late?"

It's not too late. But after 17 years of planning year-end trips, I can tell you this week is when it starts getting expensive to wait.

What I tell every family planning that week:

→ Flights first. Peak-week fares usually climb as the dates get closer. Hotels come second.
→ Pay a little extra for free cancellation. December plans change more than any other month.
→ If New Year's Eve matters to you, book that one dinner or event now. The good ones go first.
→ Come back a day before work or school restarts. Nobody regrets the buffer day.

None of this is complicated. It's just easy to put off until the options are gone.
